# Product Rules

1. Hustle is a capability-to-opportunity ecosystem, not merely a social network or freelance marketplace.
2. Every user begins as a Client.
3. Hustler and Agent are additional capabilities on the same identity.
4. There is no role switcher.
5. Content should demonstrate capability and may connect directly to services/products.
6. Trust is infrastructure: identity, verified outcomes, transactions, reviews and behaviour matter.
7. The feed exists for useful discovery, not empty engagement.
8. Experience should remain simple even when the underlying system grows complex.
